# Product
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->
## Platform
web
## Users
A Security+ learner practicing questions from their supplied PDF.
## Product Purpose
Choose a question set, answer and flag questions, submit, and inspect scored answers with the original explanations.
## Capabilities and Constraints
414 source questions grouped sequentially into sets of at most 90. Left question navigation shows answered and flagged states. Review expands individual questions. Multiple-answer questions require an exact match.
## Evidence on Hand
The supplied CompTIA Security+ SY0-701 Combined Exam Prep Q&A PDF. Content and answer keys are imported, not independently fact-checked.
## Confirmed defaults
Untimed practice, local browser persistence, four sets of 90 and one of 54. User approved direct code implementation and a calm light study layout.
## Stack
Native HTML/CSS/JavaScript with a dependency-free Node development server. No account or external service is needed.
## Product Principles
Preserve source content. Score deterministically. Keep question status visible. Reveal explanations after submission.
